4. Consent and your choices

Consent dialogue (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in a region where this is required, the app shows a consent dialogue provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform when you first start it. The dialogue implements the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF). It lets you consent to, reject or individually configure the storage of and access to information on your device and the processing of your data by Google and its advertising partners. Your choice is stored on your device.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by tapping “Privacy Settings” at the bottom of the app screen. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

The advertising identifier (IDFA) is only accessed if you allow it in the iOS “Allow app to track” dialogue. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalised using the IDFA.

Further options

In Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising you can also limit Apple’s own ad personalisation. Deleting the app removes all choices stored by the app on your device.
